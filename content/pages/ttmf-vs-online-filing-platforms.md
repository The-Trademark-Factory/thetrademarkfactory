    faq:
      - question: What is a trademark?
        answer: >-
          A trademark or brand is your unique business identifier. It lets the
          public know that the goods or services attached to the trademark are
          unique to your business. Trademark registration is therefore an
          invaluable asset and essential marketing tool for your business and
          should not be overlooked.​
      - question: Why should I register a trademark?
        answer: >-
          A registered trademark is the only way to gain exclusive rights to use
          the name or logo. Company, business and domain name registrations do
          not provide this right. Therefore, trademark registration provides
          security that you and only you own the name in relation to your
          products or services.
      - question: What are the advantages of trademark registration?
        answer: >-
          <ul><li><p><strong>Exclusivity </strong>- A registered trademark
          provides you with exclusive rights to use the name or
          logo.</p></li><li><p><strong>Australia wide coverage</strong> - An
          Australian registered trademark provides you with protection
          throughout Australia.</p></li><li><p><strong>A valuable business
          asset</strong> - A registered trademark is an important business
          asset. As with any property, a trademark can be bought and
          sold.</p></li><li><p><strong>More control of your brand </strong>- A
          registered trademark allows you to license the use of your brand much
          easier than an unregistered trademark.</p></li><li><p><strong>The
          right to stop others from using your brand</strong> - With a
          registered trademark, you can stop someone from using your brand
          without authorisation more quickly and easily that without a trademark
          registration.</p></li><li><p><strong>The right to use the ®
          symbol</strong> - Once your trademark is registered, you can use this
          symbol which puts others on notice of your rights.</p></li></ul>
      - question: How do you register a trademark in Australia?
        answer: >-
          A trademark is registered with the relevant government authority in
          each country. In Australia this authority is IP Australia.
      - question: Will an Australian trademark protect my brand in other countries?
        answer: >-
          No. Trademark rights are jurisdictional, meaning you need to register
          your trademark in each and every country you want to use the
          mark.<p>As for your Australian trademark, it protects you
          Australia-wide even if you only use your trademark in certain
          areas.</p><p>If you need international trademark protection, get in
          touch for a personalised quote.</p>
      - question: How long does it take to register a trademark in Australia?
        answer: >-
          It takes no less than 7 months from the date you file your application
          for your trademark to be registered in Australia.
      - question: How long does a trademark registration last in Australia?
        answer: >-
          Once registered, a trademark lasts 10 years from the filing date of
          the application. It can be renewed every 10 years after that provided
          you pay the renewal fees. Just remember that trademarks can become
          vulnerable to removal if they remain unused for a certain period of
          time.
      - question: How can I register my trademark overseas?
        answer: >-
          There are 2 ways to register your trade mark overseas. You can file
          your trademark nationally in each country via their government
          trademark agency OR you can file your mark via the international
          registration system known as the Madrid Protocol (provided you have an
          existing application in a Madrid Protocol (such as Australia)
          country). If you need help with international trademark registration,
          get in touch with us.
