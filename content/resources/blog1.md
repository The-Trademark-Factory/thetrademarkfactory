## **What Is a Trade Mark?**

A trade mark is anything that distinguishes your business from others. In Australia, this could include:

* A business name
* A logo or design
* A slogan or tagline
* A unique product name
* Even sounds, shapes, or colours (in some cases)

Once registered, a trade mark gives you exclusive legal rights to use that mark and the power to stop others from using it without permission.

## **Why Register a Trade Mark in Australia?**

If you’re doing business in Australia, registering a trade mark means:

* Legal ownership of your brand identity
* Protection from copycats and brand theft
* Stronger business value (especially for franchising, licensing, or selling)
* A clear signal to the market: your brand is protected

Without registration, you could spend years building a brand—only to be forced to change it or defend it at your own expense.
